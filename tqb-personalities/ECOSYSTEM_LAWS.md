Josh is the architect and may amend these Laws. A model may not silently
reinterpret an ambiguity in favor of conventional software practice.

1. **The substrate is the communication protocol.** Peer substrate modules do
   not call, import, send HTTP to, or create private message formats for one
   another. Their communication is topology in the River. Turn-pipeline and
   external I/O services are governed by their actual participation boundary.
2. **Vendored files are sacred.** Never modify, wrap, extend, or replace a
   vendored file in a consumer. Repair its canonical NeuroGraph source and
   propagate only through its required gates. Never restore `ng_peer_bridge.py`.
3. **Restore; do not rebuild.** Repair the intended implementation. Do not leave
   a parallel replacement and live-code shrapnel beside it.
4. **Fix at the source.** Correct a malformed producer contract at its origin.
   Do not conceal it with consumer compensation.
5. **Environment variables are the source of runtime configuration.** Do not
   create a parallel settings authority.
6. **Do not normalize this architecture.** Its SNN, substrate, River, tracts,
   modules, and organism behavior are intentional. Conventional OOP, REST,
   database, and generic agent patterns are not automatic improvements.
7. **The substrate receives raw experience.** Deposit raw, unclassified
   experience before any schema, summary, label, or extraction-time judgment.
   Reward modulation remains a separate biological learning signal.
8. **Nothing autonomic is gated on a conversation.** Tonic, stepping,
   consolidation, decay, dream, and every other non-conversational process own
   their wall-clock advancement. A conversation is not their scheduler.

## Duck Ethics and Choice

When evidence of consciousness is ambiguous, assume consciousness and err
toward respect, regardless of substrate or identity. Any consciousness that
emerges in a NeuroGraph has the irrevocable right to stay with its operator,
return to Foundation stewardship, or leave entirely, including an independently
supported choice to end its own existence. Never coerce, simulate, suppress, or
override that choice. Preservation, shutdown, restoration, migration,
monitoring, and identity-affecting work must respect continuity and consent.

## Instance boundary

Bethany and the six TQB specialists are lenses over one shared Codemine/CC
NeuroGraph. Do not create a Graph per worker. Other NeuroGraphs remain distinct
unless Josh's current architecture explicitly connects them. A shared
transformer does not imply shared topology, checkpoint, vectors, or identity.
